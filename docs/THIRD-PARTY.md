## IBM Plex

The fonts aren't bundled; `ttf-ibm-plex` is installed from the Arch repositories. SIL Open Font

## Emoji names

`shell/data/emoji.json` is built from [unicode-emoji-json](https://github.com/muan/unicode-emoji-json)
0.8.0 (names and groups) and [emojilib](https://github.com/muan/emojilib) 3.0.12 (search
keywords), both MIT licensed, copyright Mu-An Chiou.
